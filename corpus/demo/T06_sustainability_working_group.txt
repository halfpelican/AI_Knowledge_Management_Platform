Aisha: Thanks for coming, everyone. This is the first meeting of the sustainability working group, so I'd like to agree three things today: single-use cups, water refill stations, and how we handle electronic waste.

Owen: On cups, I've looked at the numbers. Our kitchens go through roughly two thousand single-use coffee cups a week, and none of them can go in the mixed recycling.

Aisha: Then I propose we stop supplying them. Can we remove single-use cups from all office kitchens from 1 March 2027? That gives people four months to bring their own.

Grace: I support that, as long as there are spare reusable cups for visitors. Reception could keep a box of them.

Aisha: Agreed. Single-use cups go from every kitchen on 1 March 2027, and reception keeps reusable cups for visitors. Daniel, can you draft the announcement to staff?

Daniel: Yes. I'll send it out in the first week of December, with a reminder in February.

Owen: On water, I'd like a refill station on every floor. I've asked two suppliers for quotes and expect them by the end of next week.

Grace: Can the stations be installed before summer? The tap water on level four is slow to run cold.

Owen: If the quotes come in on time, installation can be done by the end of January.

Aisha: Good. Owen owns the refill stations, with installation by the end of January.

Grace: For electronic waste, I suggest a collection day every quarter. Staff bring old cables, chargers and small devices from home, and an accredited recycler takes them away. The first collection day would be 14 February.

Daniel: Should we measure how much waste we produce now, so we can show the difference later?

Aisha: Yes. Daniel, please run a four-week waste audit starting next Monday, and report back at our next meeting.

Aisha: To summarise the actions. Daniel drafts the cups announcement for the first week of December and runs the four-week waste audit. Owen gets the refill station quotes and plans installation for the end of January. Grace books the recycler for the 14 February collection day. We meet again in four weeks.
