OFFICE RELOCATION ANNOUNCEMENT

The move
The Melbourne office will relocate to a new building on the other side of the river. The Driftwood Lane move weekend is scheduled for the second weekend of November, and the office will reopen on the following Monday.

What staff need to do
Pack your personal belongings into the crates delivered to each desk by the Thursday before the move. Label each crate with your name and your new floor. Do not pack computers; the IT team will move and reconnect them.

Working during the move
Everyone will work from home on the Friday before the move. The service desk will be available by phone as usual.

Questions
Direct questions to the facilities team. A weekly update will be published on the intranet until the move is complete.

Additional guidance
Questions about office relocation announcement can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance. Staff are expected to follow the arrangements for office relocation announcement and to ask their manager when they are unsure how a rule applies to their situation. Exceptions to office relocation announcement require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit.

Review and ownership
Exceptions to office relocation announcement require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit. New starters are introduced to office relocation announcement during induction, and a reminder is issued whenever the arrangements change. Where office relocation announcement affects customers or the public, the responsible team must make sure the wording is clear, consistent and easy to find.
