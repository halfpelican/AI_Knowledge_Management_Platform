MEETING ROOM BOOKING RULES

Booking
Meeting rooms are booked through the shared calendar. Please book only the time you need and cancel promptly if the meeting is cancelled so that others can use the room.

Etiquette
The Granite Pier booking etiquette asks everyone to start and finish on time, leave the room tidy, wipe the whiteboards and return chairs to their places. If you arrive and the room is occupied, check the calendar before asking people to leave.

Equipment
Each room has a display, a conference phone and a video camera. Faults should be reported to the service desk with the room name.

Large events
Bookings of more than twenty people or bookings that run past six in the evening must be approved by the facilities team at least one week in advance.

Additional guidance
The owner of meeting room booking rules reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change. New starters are introduced to meeting room booking rules during induction, and a reminder is issued whenever the arrangements change. Staff are expected to follow the arrangements for meeting room booking rules and to ask their manager when they are unsure how a rule applies to their situation.

Review and ownership
Exceptions to meeting room booking rules require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit. Where meeting room booking rules affects customers or the public, the responsible team must make sure the wording is clear, consistent and easy to find. Questions about meeting room booking rules can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance.
