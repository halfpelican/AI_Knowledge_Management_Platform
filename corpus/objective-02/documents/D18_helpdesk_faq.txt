IT HELPDESK FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I forgot my password
Use the self-service page to reset it. The Nickel Bay password reset window is open between seven in the morning and eight in the evening on working days. Outside those hours, contact the service desk.

My laptop is slow
Restart the laptop at least once a week and close applications you are not using. If it is still slow, log a ticket so a technician can check the disk and memory.

How do I connect to the printer?
Select the nearest printer from the list on your laptop and enter your staff card number at the printer to release your job.

Who do I call?
The service desk is open from seven in the morning to six in the evening. Urgent faults outside those hours can be reported by email and will be triaged the next working morning.
