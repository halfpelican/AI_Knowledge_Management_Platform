FLEET VEHICLE USAGE NOTES

Who may drive
Only employees with a current licence who have completed the driver induction may use a fleet vehicle. Licence details are checked every year.

Logbooks
The Coral Ridge logbook rule requires the driver to record the date, start and end odometer readings, destination and purpose of every trip. Logbooks are collected monthly by the fleet coordinator.

Fuel and maintenance
Vehicles are refuelled using the fuel card in the glovebox. Report warning lights, unusual noises or damage to the fleet coordinator on the same day.

Private use
Fleet vehicles may not be used for private trips except where a written exception has been approved by the department head.

Additional guidance
Exceptions to fleet vehicle usage notes require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit. Questions about fleet vehicle usage notes can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance. The owner of fleet vehicle usage notes reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change.

Review and ownership
Where fleet vehicle usage notes affects customers or the public, the responsible team must make sure the wording is clear, consistent and easy to find. The owner of fleet vehicle usage notes reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change.
