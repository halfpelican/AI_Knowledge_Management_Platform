ACCESS CARD REPLACEMENT PROCEDURE

When to use this procedure
Follow these steps when a staff access card is lost, stolen, damaged or stops working. A card that has been lost or stolen is a security risk until it is deactivated, so step 1 is urgent.

Steps
1. Report the loss to the security desk by phone or in person within one hour of noticing it. Give your name, your department and where the card was last used. The security desk deactivates the card immediately under the Ironbark deactivation rule.
2. Complete the access card request form on the intranet, selecting "replacement" and the reason. Attach a note from your manager if the card was lost for the second time in twelve months.
3. Visit the security desk with photographic identification. A temporary visitor badge is issued for the day if the replacement cannot be printed at once.
4. Collect the replacement card from the security desk. Cards are printed between nine in the morning and four in the afternoon on working days and are usually ready within two hours of the form being received.
5. Test the new card on your floor's entry door and on the lift. Report any failure to the security desk the same day.
6. If the original card is later found, hand it to the security desk for destruction. Do not keep it, because a deactivated card cannot be reactivated.

Fees
The first replacement in a calendar year is free. A second replacement costs twenty dollars, recovered through payroll with your consent.

Additional guidance
Staff are expected to follow the arrangements for the access card replacement procedure and to ask their manager when they are unsure how a rule applies to their situation. Questions about the procedure can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance.

Review and ownership
The owner of the access card replacement procedure reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change.
