RECRUITMENT ACTIVITY REPORT, THIRD QUARTER 2026

Summary
Twenty-six vacancies were opened in the quarter and twenty-one were filled. The median time to hire was thirty-four days, four days faster than last quarter and inside the forty-day target for the first time this year. Agency spend fell by a third as more roles were filled through referrals and direct advertising.

Activity
Measure | This quarter | Last quarter
Vacancies opened | 26 | 31
Vacancies filled | 21 | 24
Median time to hire (days) | 34 | 38
Offers declined | 3 | 6
Agency spend | $41,000 | $62,000

Sources of hire
Seven of the twenty-one hires came through the Wattle Grove employee referral scheme, eight through direct advertising, four through agencies and two were internal transfers. Referral hires continue to have the highest offer acceptance rate, at ninety-five percent.

Findings
The five vacancies still open at quarter end are all in the technology team, where two offers were declined on salary. The roles that took longest to fill were those advertised without a salary range. Candidate feedback on the interview process was positive, with the exception of the time taken to confirm interview dates.

Diversity
Forty-eight percent of applicants and fifty-two percent of hires were women. Applications from candidates aged over fifty rose from nine to fourteen percent after the advertising wording was changed.

Next quarter
Twelve vacancies are already approved for the fourth quarter, including the five carried over. The people team will publish a salary range on every advertisement and will trial scheduling interviews through the shared calendar to cut confirmation times.

Prepared by the people and culture team, 7 October 2026.
