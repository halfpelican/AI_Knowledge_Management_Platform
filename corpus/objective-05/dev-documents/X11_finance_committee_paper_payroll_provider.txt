FINANCE COMMITTEE
Meeting of 23 September 2026, paper 2

OPTIONS FOR A NEW PAYROLL PROVIDER

Purpose of this paper
The contract with the current payroll provider expires on 30 June 2027. This paper sets out the options for the service from July 2027 and seeks the committee's agreement to the recommended option and to the procurement that follows.

Recommendation
That the committee agree to run an open tender for a managed payroll service, option B, with the evaluation weighted sixty percent on service quality and forty percent on price, and that the result be brought back to the committee in February 2027.

Background
Payroll for 620 staff is processed fortnightly by an external provider under a five-year contract. Service has been adequate, but three pay runs in the past year contained errors affecting more than ten people, and the Marigold self-service portal the provider offers has not been adopted because it does not meet the organisation's accessibility standard.

Options
Option A: renew with the current provider for three years at a quoted increase of eight percent. Lowest effort, but no remedy for the service issues.
Option B: open tender for a managed payroll service. Allows the accessibility standard and error-rate targets to be written into the contract. Estimated procurement cost of forty staff days.
Option C: bring payroll in-house. Requires two additional staff and a payroll system licence; estimated to cost twelve percent more than option B over five years, with the organisation carrying all compliance risk.

Timeline
Tender published in October 2026, evaluation in December, committee decision in February 2027, transition from April, first pay run with the new provider in July 2027.

Decision sought
The committee is asked to agree to option B and to the timeline above.

Author: financial controller. Sponsor: chief financial officer.
