CUSTOMER SATISFACTION REPORT, THIRD QUARTER 2026

Summary
This report presents the results of the quarterly customer satisfaction survey for July to September 2026. Overall satisfaction rose to eighty-four percent, up three points on the previous quarter, and the Kingfisher net promoter score reached thirty-one. Response times remain the strongest driver of satisfaction and billing clarity the weakest.

Method
The survey was sent to every customer contact who raised a support case or renewed a contract during the quarter: 1,240 people. 384 responded, a response rate of thirty-one percent, compared with twenty-eight percent last quarter. Scores are weighted by customer size.

Findings
Satisfaction with support response times was ninety-one percent, the highest figure recorded since the survey began. Satisfaction with the clarity of invoices was sixty-two percent, unchanged. Thirty-eight written comments mentioned the new online portal, of which thirty were positive. Customers in the smallest size band gave the lowest scores on every question.

Results by question
Question | This quarter | Last quarter
Overall satisfaction | 84% | 81%
Support response times | 91% | 86%
Quality of answers | 83% | 82%
Clarity of invoices | 62% | 62%
Likelihood to recommend | 31 (net score) | 26 (net score)

Trends
Overall satisfaction has risen for three consecutive quarters. The gap between the largest and smallest customers has widened from six points to nine.

Next steps
The billing team will trial a redesigned invoice with twenty customers in November and report the effect in the fourth-quarter survey. The customer success team will contact every small-band customer who gave a score below sixty percent.

Prepared by the customer insight team, 9 October 2026.
