CAR PARK RESURFACING ANNOUNCEMENT

The work
The staff car park will be resurfaced over the Easter long weekend. The contractor will strip the old surface on the Thursday evening and lay the new surface over the following four days, and the car park will reopen on the Tuesday morning.

What staff need to know
The car park will be closed from six in the evening on the Thursday. Cars left in the car park after that time will be moved to the street at the owner's cost. The bicycle racks at the side entrance are not affected.

During the closure
Staff who need to drive in may use the public car park on Wattle Street, and receipts for the closure days can be claimed back. The shuttle from the railway station runs as usual.

Questions
Direct questions to the facilities team. A notice will be posted in the lobby when the car park has reopened.

Additional guidance
Questions about car park resurfacing announcement can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance. Exceptions to car park resurfacing announcement require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit.
