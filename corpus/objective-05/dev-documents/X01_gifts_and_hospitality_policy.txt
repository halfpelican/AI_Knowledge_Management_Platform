GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY POLICY

Purpose
This policy sets out when staff may accept or offer gifts, meals, tickets and other hospitality in the course of their work, and how such offers are recorded. It applies to every employee, contractor and board member.

Thresholds
A gift or hospitality with a value under fifty dollars may be accepted and must be noted in the Bramble Gate gifts register within five working days. Anything valued between fifty and two hundred dollars may be accepted only with the written approval of the department head, recorded against the register entry. Anything valued above two hundred dollars must be declined, or handed to the organisation where declining would cause offence. Cash, vouchers and gift cards are never accepted, whatever their value.

Offering hospitality
Hospitality offered to clients or suppliers must be modest, proportionate and approved in advance by a department head. It must never be offered during a tender, a contract negotiation or a dispute with the recipient's organisation.

Conflicts
Staff involved in selecting or managing a supplier may not accept any gift or hospitality from that supplier while the arrangement is being decided or reviewed.

Breaches
A breach of this policy is a disciplinary matter. Failing to record a gift is itself a breach, even where the gift could have been accepted.

Additional guidance
The owner of the gifts and hospitality policy reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change. Questions about the policy can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance.

Review and ownership
Exceptions to the gifts and hospitality policy require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit. New starters are introduced to the policy during induction, and a reminder is issued whenever the arrangements change.
