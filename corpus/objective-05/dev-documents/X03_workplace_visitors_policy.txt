WORKPLACE VISITORS POLICY

Purpose
This policy sets the rules for bringing visitors, contractors and family members into the organisation's offices, so that the people in the building are known, safe and accounted for in an emergency.

Rules
Every visitor signs in at reception, is issued a Copperleaf visitor badge and wears it visibly for the whole visit. A visitor must be met at reception by the member of staff hosting them and must be accompanied by a member of staff at all times beyond the reception area. Visitors may not be given a staff access card, a network login or a key.

Contractors
Contractors who will work unaccompanied must first complete the contractor induction and sign the site rules. Their access is limited to the floors named on their work order and expires on the date the order ends.

After hours
Visitors are not permitted outside the hours of seven in the morning to seven in the evening without the prior written approval of the facilities manager.

Responsibilities
The host is responsible for the visitor's conduct and for ensuring they sign out and return the badge. Reception keeps the visitor record for twelve months.

Additional guidance
New starters are introduced to the workplace visitors policy during induction, and a reminder is issued whenever the arrangements change. Questions about the policy can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance.

Review and ownership
The owner of the workplace visitors policy reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change. Exceptions to the policy require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit.
