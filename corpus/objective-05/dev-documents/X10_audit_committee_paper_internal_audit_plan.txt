AUDIT COMMITTEE
Meeting of 11 November 2026, paper 5

INTERNAL AUDIT PLAN FOR 2027

Purpose of this paper
This paper presents the proposed internal audit plan for the 2027 calendar year for the committee's approval, and sets out how the proposed audits were selected and resourced.

Recommendation
That the committee approve the plan of eight audits set out below, note the resourcing assumptions, and agree that the head of internal audit may reorder the audits during the year with the chair's agreement.

How the plan was built
Each area of the organisation was rated for risk using the Sandpiper risk model: the inherent risk of the activity, the time since it was last audited, and the findings still open. The eight highest-rated areas form the plan. Three areas were added at management's request and two at the committee's.

Proposed audits
Quarter 1: supplier onboarding and payments; access to the human resources system.
Quarter 2: project governance for the knowledge platform; expense claims.
Quarter 3: cyber security incident response; records retention and disposal.
Quarter 4: payroll changes; follow-up of findings open for more than a year.

Resourcing
The plan needs 410 audit days. The team has 360 days available after training and leave, so fifty days will be bought from the co-source provider under the existing panel arrangement, at a cost of $62,000, within the approved budget.

Matters for the committee's attention
Eleven findings from 2025 remain open, of which four are rated high. The fourth-quarter follow-up audit will report on every one of them.

Decision sought
The committee is asked to approve the 2027 internal audit plan and the co-source spend.

Author: head of internal audit.
