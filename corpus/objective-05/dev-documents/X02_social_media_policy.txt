SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY

Scope
This policy covers the use of social media by employees and contractors, whether on personal accounts or on accounts the organisation operates, and whether during or outside working hours. It applies wherever a post could reasonably be connected with the organisation.

Speaking for the organisation
Only staff authorised by the communications team may post on the organisation's own accounts or speak publicly on its behalf. Requests from journalists or on public forums are referred to the communications team on the same day.

Personal accounts
Staff may identify their employer on a personal account but must make clear that their views are their own. Confidential information, unreleased figures, client details and images of colleagues taken at work must not be posted. The Silver Fern conduct standard applies online exactly as it applies in the office: posts must be lawful, respectful and free of harassment.

Security
Work passwords must never be reused on social media accounts. Any message that appears to come from the organisation but was not sent by it must be reported to the service desk.

Breaches
A post that breaches this policy may be required to be removed, and a serious breach is a disciplinary matter.

Additional guidance
Staff are expected to follow the arrangements for the social media policy and to ask their manager when they are unsure how a rule applies to their situation. The owner of the social media policy reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change.

Review and ownership
Exceptions to the social media policy require written approval and are kept on file so that they can be checked during an audit. Where the policy affects customers or the public, the responsible team must make sure the wording is clear, consistent and easy to find.
