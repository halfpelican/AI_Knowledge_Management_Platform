MEETING-FREE FRIDAY GUIDELINES

Purpose
These guidelines protect one day a week for focused work. From the first Friday in February, no internal meetings may be scheduled on Fridays.

The rule
Internal meetings, stand-ups and one-to-ones must not be booked on a Friday. Calendar invitations for internal meetings on a Friday are declined automatically by the scheduling assistant. Meetings with customers, suppliers or regulators are allowed, because their timing is not ours to set.

Exceptions
An urgent incident review may be held on a Friday when service to customers is affected. The person calling it records the reason in the incident log. Repeated exceptions in one team are raised with the department head.

Expectations
Staff may still reply to messages on Fridays, but nobody is expected to respond to a non-urgent message before Monday. Managers set the example by keeping their own Fridays free.

Additional guidance
Staff are expected to follow the arrangements for meeting-free friday guidelines and to ask their manager when they are unsure how a rule applies to their situation. Questions about meeting-free friday guidelines can be raised with the relevant team through the intranet, and answers of general interest are added to the guidance.

Review and ownership
The owner of meeting-free friday guidelines reviews it at least once a year and records any change, together with the date and the reason for the change.
